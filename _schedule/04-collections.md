## Discussion Topic

_How do digital libraries, digitized historical archives, and born-digital archives intersection with the digital humanities? Or: how do LIS and DH conversations contribute to each other, and where to they diverge?_

### Core

+ Thomas Padilla, "On a Collections as Data Imperative" (2017), [external resource](https://www.digitalpreservation.gov/meetings/dcs16/tpadilla_OnaCollectionsasDataImperative_final.pdf)
+ Rachel Wittmann, Anna Neatrour, Rebekah Cummings, and Jeremy Myntti, “From Digital Library to Open Datasets: Embracing a ‘Collections As Data’ Framework” (2019), [external resource](https://doi.org/10.6017/ital.v38i4.11101)
+ Matthew G. Kirschenbaum, "Archives Without Dust" from _Bitstreams_ (2021), [library resource](https://doi-org.proxy2.library.illinois.edu/10.2307/j.ctv1f45qbq.5)
